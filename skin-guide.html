Clinical Fact Check

Debunking Sun Protection Myths

Misinformation leads to 80% of preventable premature skin aging and cutaneous DNA damage.

Myth #1

"I don't need sunscreen indoors or on cloudy days."

Dermatologist Truth

UVA rays penetrate standard office window glass and cloud cover with zero loss of intensity. UVA breaks down collagen silently without causing heat or redness.

Myth #2

"Wearing sunscreen blocks my body from making Vitamin D."

Dermatologist Truth

Clinical studies show that even with consistent daily SPF use, your skin synthesizes adequate Vitamin D. It only takes 10 minutes of incidental sunlight on arms.

Myth #3

"Darker skin tones have natural immunity to solar damage."

Dermatologist Truth

Melanin provides a natural SPF of ~13, which is insufficient. Darker skin is especially prone to hyperpigmentation, melasma, and late-stage melanoma diagnoses.
