Will SOLVÉA sunscreens leave a white chalky cast on dark skin?
No. Our flagship Hydro-Shield Invisible Fluid SPF 50+ is formulated with transparent photostable micro-filters that dry down 100% undetectable on all Fitzpatrick skin types (I through VI). We also offer the Sol-Chroma Tinted Mineral Shield for enhanced universal tone blending.

Yes. Every SOLVÉA product complies strictly with the Hawaii and Palau Coral Reef Protection Acts. We are 100% free from Oxybenzone, Octinoxate, Octocrylene, 4-Methylbenzylidene Camphor, and synthetic microplastics.
